# Limitations

Stated plainly, because several of them bound what the results can mean.

## Of the evidence itself
- **The texts are very short**: median 4-5 signs, longest about 17. No decipherment has
  ever been achieved from material this short without a bilingual.
- **There is no bilingual**, and the language is unknown. Script, language and content are
  all unknown at once, unlike Linear B where only the script was.
- **The sign inventory (roughly 400-700) is too large for a syllabary**, so many signs must
  carry word-level meanings that distribution alone cannot recover.
- **Depth is not a calendar.** Level below datum records how much debris had accumulated,
  not intention. Seals can be intrusive from above or redeposited from below, and the
  phase labels are averages of floor levels, not dated horizons.
- **Mackay and Marshall depths are not poolable**: different datums and different locus
  resolution.

## Of the corpora
- **Sources disagree on 10-20% of sign positions** (FINDINGS 14).
- **Sign numbering is our own** where the G-numbered corpus is used; the standard lists are
  Mahadevan's and Parpola's.
- **Near-duplicate inscriptions** inflate any uncorrected statistic; `first_occurrence` is
  used throughout, and one early result changed when it was applied (FINDINGS 2).
- **The digitisation records mostly single lines** and does not record object faces, so the
  question of whether one object carries several inscriptions is open (FINDINGS 24, 26).

## Of the coding
- **One coder.** The reliability check (kappa 0.89, sign counts within one) measures
  consistency, not accuracy. A second person might read the photographs differently.
- **Fish and arrow signs are not reliably readable** at plate resolution and are recorded
  as "likely"/"unclear" rather than counted.
- **Plate numbers were read by eye** because the hand-lettered digits defeat OCR; one
  assignment error was found and corrected during a spot check (No. 1 -> No. 40).

## Of the analysis
- **Exploratory, not preregistered.** Hypotheses were formed while looking at the data.
  Where a marginal result later evaporated, both figures are reported (FINDINGS 23).
- **Multiple comparisons** are not formally corrected; marginal p-values should be read
  with that in mind, and one such result did vanish with more data.
- **Most script-internal findings replicate published work** (see PRIOR_WORK.md).
- **One positive result failed to replicate** on independent data (FINDINGS 26), and is
  marked retired rather than removed.
- **High conditioning does not imply syntax**: in Egyptian, where the answer is known,
  sign-level conditioning exceeds grammatical-category conditioning (FINDINGS 36). Our
  Indus figures should be read with that calibration in mind.
