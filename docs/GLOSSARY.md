# Glossary

**Behaviour class** - a grouping of signs by where they occur in a text (openers, text-final
markers, strict medial connectors, numerals...), derived from position statistics, not shape.

**CISI** - *Corpus of Indus Seals and Inscriptions* (Joshi & Parpola 1987-), the standard
edition. Its numbering (M-nnn for Mohenjo-daro) is used by most digitisations.

**Conditioning excess** - how much of the uncertainty in one element is removed by its
neighbour, above what the same corpus gives when shuffled, as a share of that element's
entropy. Used for all cross-corpus comparisons because raw mutual information is
upward-biased in small samples.

**Determinative** - in Egyptian, an unpronounced sign at the end of a word marking its
category. Identified here distributionally (word-final, attaching to many unrelated words).

**DK area, SD area, HR, VS...** - excavation areas at Mohenjo-daro. DK and SD are Mackay's;
HR, VS and the rest appear in Marshall's table.

**First occurrence** - the deduplication flag. Many inscriptions are near-duplicates; all
analyses use one representative per group.

**G-numbers (G740, G520...)** - sign identifiers in the digitised corpus used here. Not a
standard list: G740 is the "jar", G520 the "arrow".

**Jar sign (G740)** - the commonest text-final sign, ending about a third of texts and
almost never starting one.

**M77** - Mahadevan's 1977 corpus, *The Indus Script: Texts, Concordance and Tables*.

**Minimal pair** - two texts differing in exactly one position; used to test whether texts
are built by varying one slot of a shared frame.

**Phase estimate** - a rough chronological band derived from level. Approximate; see the
depth caveat in LIMITATIONS.

**Reading direction** - right to left, as the script runs. On an impression the reading
order therefore runs from the right edge, so a text's ENDING appears at the left.

**Register** - here, the distinction between the main line of an inscription and the short
formulaic second line found on about a fifth of them.

**Type B, F, C, D...** - Mackay's seal shapes. B is the square seal with a perforated boss
(the classic animal seal); F is the rectangular inscription-only seal.
