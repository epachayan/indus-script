# Mackay (1938), Further Excavations at Mohenjo-daro, vol. I, ch. XI
## "Tabulation of Seals (DK and SD Areas)" - hand transcription

Source: Internet Archive scan TVA_BOK_0009130 (out of copyright). `pdf_page` is the page
number within the chapter-XI extract (page 45 = printed p. 369); printed page = pdf_page + 324.

Transcribed by reading the page images (OCR was too unreliable on these tables).

Conventions
- `dims_in`: as printed, in inches, `?` where Mackay prints `?`.
- `level_ft`: feet relative to the DK datum (SD-area seals relative to the SD bench mark,
  2.2 ft higher); `surface` where printed "Surface".
- `street`: filled when the locus is a street or "Bet. Bls. ..." instead of block/house/room.
- Ditto marks (") are resolved to the value above.
- Types (Mackay p. 325, following Marshall 1931): A cylinder; B square with perforated
  boss; C square, no boss, often two-sided; D rectangular, no boss, often two-sided;
  E button with linear designs; F rectangular with perforated convex back; ... ; `?` as printed.

Checked anomalies (printed that way in the book)
- Nos. 48, 71 and 149 are not in the table.
- Field no. DK 12318 is printed for both No. 108 and No. 196 (different loci): printing error.
- Nos. 199/212 (DK 3649), 236/248 (DK 12286) and 280/285 (DK 5575) have the same field
  number, size and locus: the same seal appears to be listed twice.

- Nos. 199/212 ... see above; also DK 5941 (394/400), DK 7269 (542/544) and DK 5636
  (552/553) are listed twice with the same locus. DK 5580 is printed for No. 354
  (level -11.6) and No. 642 (level -20.0), same locus: one level is a misprint.
- Addenda repeating earlier seals: 378A (= 30), 378B/378C (= 229), 378D (= 376),
  686A (= 430), 686B (= 494), 686C; 604B is an extra seal.
- Levels that break the table's depth order, as printed: 374 (sealing, -10.1),
  412 (-16.8), 430 (-14.9), 537 (-23.6); Nos. 684-686 (-25.4 to -30.5) are the deepest
  seals; 687-692 come from the trench north of the DK area (field nos. DK.H.), 693-704
  from the SD area (levels on the SD bench mark).
- Materials: after No. 520 (Silver) and No. 551 (Faience) the book prints ditto marks
  down the column until "Steatite" is printed again. Taken literally, Nos. 521-539
  would be silver and 552-572 faience. Recorded as "ditto after ... (as printed)";
  treat with caution.
- The glyph used for "?" in this font resembles "I" or "!"; No. 372's type is "?".

Validation
- Mackay's own chapter text gives 558 type-B and 81 type-F seals; the transcription has
  559 and 84.
- His lists of rare types agree with the table except where the book disagrees with
  itself (checked against the scan): No. 73 is F in the table but D in the text,
  No. 499 is F in the table but C in the text, and Nos. 36, 383 (E), 449 (C), 509 (A),
  528, 542, 544, 699 (D) have those types in the table but are missing from the text lists.
- Depths of Nos. 695, 697, 698, 701 and 703 match the chapter-II narrative.
- The "Pashupati" seal (No. 420) matches its published description: Block 1, -12.8 ft
  (3.9 m), 1.4 x 1.39 in (3.56 x 3.53 cm).
